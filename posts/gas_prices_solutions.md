# Discussion

[this 100L backpack costs less than $20 thru *Amazon*](https://www.amazon.com/Camping-Backpack-Capacity-Waterproof-Outdoor/dp/B0H7391WZ3), which stores around 4' cubic (which is the average car trunk use for trips to the store).

[this scooter goes up to 16**mph**, for just $200 thru *Amazon*, suitable if your town has bicycle paths to the store](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0FXZZZJCV). do not merge into the traffic, since this scooter does not go as fast as cars, plus has no turn signals to use.

[this 1000w cargo bicycle is $300 thru *Amazon*](https://www.amazon.com/VARUN-Electric-All-Terrain-Removable-Suspension/dp/B0H3LB659Y); does not include cargo baskets, but includes cargo rack on the back to [mount storage onto](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0BYRMN21J), plus the front column is compatible with most [commuter storage addons](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=bicycle+front+cargo).

[this scooter goes up to 30**mph**, plus includes turn signals (you should attach mirrors if you use roads though, plus ensure you are comfortable going as fast as the cars), for $360 thru *Amazon*](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0FX3R2H6D). numerous [cargo addons](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=scooter+cargo) (so you have less load on your shoulders, or for if your backpack does not have enough room) are also available thru *Amazon*.

[this cargo scooter for $2000 thru *Amazon* has as much room in the back as cars do, plus includes mirrors & turn signals (required for road use)](https://www.amazon.com/60-70Km-Electric-Tricycle-Capacity-Battery/dp/B0GFSRZL82/)

[this skateboard goes 29**mph**, suitable if your town has bicycle paths to the store, for $160 thru *Amazon*](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DF7GN9L9?ref_=ppx_hzsearch_conn_dt_b_fed_asin_title_43). do not merge into traffic on skateboards though.

*Amazon* also has numerous gas-powered mopeds [such as this 150**CC** moped advertised as 45**mph** (which one review says goes up to 55**mph** if the load is small) for $700, which includes mirrors&turn signals](https://www.amazon.com/HHH-150cc-Street-Aluminum-Wheels/), but mopeds weigh a lot more (hurt more if you fall) plus cost a lot more, plus require you to purchase gas (although versus a car, the gas use is low).

do not go faster than electric wheelchairs if you go on sidewalks with pedestrians.

always use [motorcross pads](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=motorcross+pads) on vulnerable portions of you when you are on the road, as much as you would use on powered bicycles/motorcycles which go however fast your scooter goes.

ensure to follow all of your jurisdiction's road rules for mopeds if you go on the roads; most jurisdictions reuse the rules for mopeds, for scooters.
