**[fēl-is pisc-em cēpit "the cat caught the fish" to fēl-em pisc-is cēpit "the fish caught the cat"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analytic_language#:~:text=fish). Human ancestors are fish.**

It is common to insult [those who confuse](https://www.answers.com/mammals/Why_do_people_think_that_dolphins_were_fish) [_delphinida_](https://wikipedia.org/wiki/Delphinida) ([porpoises](https://duckduckgo.com/?t=h_&q=horse%20sims), [dolphins](https://www.dingogames.com/dolphin/) or orcas) with fish (since _delphinida_ are mammals, evolved from cows — for biologists; “[_artiodactyls_](https://wikipedia.org/wiki/Artiodactyl)”, which is the group which includes both _delphinida_ + cows), but perhaps you should not; [all tetrapods (and thus all mammals) evolved from the lobe-finned fish](https://wikipedia.org/wiki/Evolution_of_tetrapods).
- Most fish organs are also human organs. [Most human organs are also fish organs](https://www.mathsisfun.com/associative-commutative-distributive.html).
- Both have mouths, tongues, throats, stomachs, intestines, [bladder](https://www.deviantart.com/dacos95/art/underwater-world-A-wip6-1223583458)s, [anus](https://www.hyperphysics.phy-astr.gsu.edu/hbase/Solar/uranus.html)es.
- Both have [hearts](https://www.deviantart.com/dacos95/art/underwater-world-A-wip6-1223583458).
- Both have [eyes](https://duckduckgo.com/?t=h_&q=mice&ia=images&iax=images), [ears](https://i.etsystatic.com/41632471/r/il/94566a/4984509254/il_fullxfull.4984509254_m7lz.jpg), [skin](https://www.robloxsticker.com/skins), [peripheral nervous systems](https://poe.com/s/LcRxFfzGrHEZ01taCIyl).
- Both have adaptive [central nervous systems](https://github.com/SwuduSusuwu/SusuLib/blob/preview/posts/CnsCompress.md) with [consciousness](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC543470/).

[Humans](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gg5_mlQOsUQ), [cats](https://duckduckgo.com/?t=h_&q=nekolocation&ia=web), mongoose, [cows](https://superuser.com/questions/43715/what-are-super-cow-powers-and-why-doesnt-my-aptitude-have-them), porpoises plus [dinosaurs](https://blog.en.uptodown.com/dinosaurs-pubg-mobile-details-how-they-work/) all have the [lobe-finned](https://duckduckgo.com/?t=h_&q=lotion) fish as common [ancestors](https://itsfoss.com/birth-of-unix/), so it is important that [fishers](https://wikipedia.org/wiki/Phishers) allow our [oceans](https://www.digitalocean.com/) to continue to have numerous [fish](https://bashcommands.com/fish-vs-bash)es.
